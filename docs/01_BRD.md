# Business Requirements Document (BRD) - DeskIn

## 1. Business Problem
In Egypt, freelancers, remote workers, and students face continuous challenges finding suitable workspace environments (co-working spaces, private desks, meeting rooms). Existing booking methods rely heavily on phone calls or WhatsApp messages, leading to reservation delays and double-booking issues.

## 2. Business Goal
To streamline workspace discovery and booking, providing a seamless mobile application that allows users to find, reserve, and confirm space availability in real time within minutes.

## 3. Business Rules
* **No Double-Booking:** Time slots already reserved cannot be selected by another user.
* **Auto-Cancellation:** Temporary reservations expire after 15 minutes if unconfirmed/unpaid.
* **Slot Granularity:** Desk reservations are handled on hourly/daily basis.
